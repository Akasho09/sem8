## soft fork
- A soft fork in Bitcoin is a backward-compatible protocol upgrade:
- New rules are stricter than old ones.
- Upgraded nodes enforce new rules.
- Non-upgraded nodes still accept new blocks as valid (they just don’t enforce the stricter rules).
- Quick Summary
    - Soft Fork → backward compatible ✅
    - Hard Fork → not backward compatible ❌
    - Temporary Fork → accidental, resolves automatically ⚠️
