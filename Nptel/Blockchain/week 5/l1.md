# Limitations of PoW & Alternative Consensus Mechanisms

## ⚠️ 1. Limitations of Proof of Work (PoW)
1. 🔴 (A) Trust Assumption Problem
- PoW is called trustless
- ❗ BUT actually:
    - Requires majority (>50%) nodes to be honest
- Problem:
    - Difficult for:
    - Small networks
    - Enterprise applications
- Example:
    - Supply chain blockchain → few participants
    - Easy to compromise network

## 📉 2. Low Transaction Throughput
- Bitcoin:
    - ~3–7 transactions/sec
- Ethereum:
    - ~15 transactions/sec
- Compare:
    - Visa/MasterCard:
        ~5000–10000 transactions/sec
- Conclusion:
    - ❌ PoW is too slow for real-world systems

## ⚡ 3. High Energy Consumption
- Huge computational work required
- Stats:
    - ~157 TWh/year energy usage
    - ~1737 kWh per transaction
- Impact:
    - High electricity usage
    - Large carbon footprint
- Insight:
    - Thousands mine → Only one wins → Energy wasted

## 🧠 4. Need for Alternative Consensus
- Why?
- Improve:
    - Speed
    - Efficiency
    - Scalability
    - Energy usage

## 🪙 5. Proof of Stake (PoS)
- Mining power ∝ Coins owned
- Example:
- If user owns 1% coins → mines 1% blocks
- Key Idea:
    - Replace computation → ownership
- Advantages:
    - Energy efficient
    - Harder to attack
    - Why attack is costly?
    - Attacker must own large number of coins
- Variants:
    - 🔹 Random Selection
    - 🔹 Coin Age
- Coin Age:
    - Coin Age = Coins × Holding Time
- Benefit:
    - Prevents instant attacks
- Limitation:
❌ Requires initial coin distribution
> ❌ Depends on PoW (bootstrapping issue)

## 🔥 6. Proof of Burn (PoB)
- Burn coins to gain mining power
- Burning means:
    - Send coins to unspendable address
- Idea:
    - Sacrifice coins → gain right to mine
- Advantages:
    - No energy waste
    - Fair participation
- Limitation:
> ❌ Still depends on PoW coins
❌ Permanent loss of coins

## ⏳ 7. Proof of Elapsed Time (PoET)
```yml
        Each node waits random time
                ↓
        First to finish → becomes leader
```
- Goal:
    - Replace computation with waiting time
- Problem:
    - ❗ How to verify honest waiting?

## 🖥️ 8. Solution: Trusted Execution Environment
- Technology:
    - Intel SGX (Software Guard Extensions)
- Concept:
    - Special hardware ensures:
        - Node actually waited correct time
- Working:
    - Code runs in secure hardware enclave
    - Cannot be modified
    - Provides proof of execution
- Result:
    - Trust in hardware instead of computation

## ⚙️ 9. Comparison of Consensus Mechanisms
| Mechanism | Basis        | Advantage   | Limitation         |
| --------- | ------------ | ----------- | ------------------ |
| PoW       | Computation  | Secure      | Energy waste       |
| PoS       | Coins owned  | Efficient   | Needs distribution |
| PoB       | Burn coins   | Less energy | Coin loss          |
| PoET      | Waiting time | Fast        | Needs hardware     |

## ⚖️ 10. Trade-offs in Consensus
- Important Insight:
    - No perfect consensus mechanism exists
- Trade-offs:
    - Security vs Speed
    - Decentralization vs Efficiency
    - Trust vs Performance

## 🏢 11. Enterprise Challenge
❌ PoW not suitable
- Possible solutions:
    - PoET
    - Permissioned consensus (next lectures)

# 📌 Final Summary
PoW works well for large public networks
But suffers from:
High energy
Low speed
Security assumptions
Evolution:
PoW → PoS / PoB → PoET
